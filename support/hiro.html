Common questions

I did not get my sign-in code

HIRO sends a six-digit code over WhatsApp to the number you entered. Check that the number is right, including the country code — the app defaults to India (+91), and you can change it by tapping the dial code. Check WhatsApp itself rather than SMS. If it still does not arrive, wait a minute and use Resend, then contact us.

I recorded something and it has not reached my clinic

HIRO saves what you record on your phone first, so it works with no signal. It sends when a connection returns. Profile shows whether anything is still waiting. Nothing you have recorded is lost while it waits.

My reminders are not arriving

Reminders are optional and off until you turn them on, in Profile → Reminders. If they are on and nothing arrives, check that notifications are allowed for HIRO in your phone's settings, and that a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is not silencing them. On Android, also allow HIRO Alarms & reminders in its app settings, or a reminder can arrive up to an hour late. Reminders never name a medicine or a dose, so what appears on your lock screen is deliberately vague.

A medicine on my list is wrong

Medicines your clinic has confirmed are marked as theirs, and only your clinic can change those — contact them. A medicine you added yourself can be removed from your list using the delete icon on its card.

I want to bring in readings from Apple Health or Health Connect

Track → From Apple Health (on an iPhone) or From Health Connect (on Android). HIRO shows you what it found from the last seven days, and keeps only the readings you choose. It only reads; it never writes anything back. On Android 13 and earlier, Health Connect is an app from Google Play, and HIRO offers to install it. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Apple Health or Health Connect.

How do I delete my HIRO account?

In the app: Profile → Close your account. This removes your sign-in from every device immediately.

Without the app, for example if you no longer have the phone: email support@m16labs.com with the subject Delete my HIRO account and the phone number you sign in with. We confirm the request with you before we act on it, and we reply to tell you when the account is closed.

What is deleted, and what is kept. Closing your account ends your sign-in, and data held only for optional purposes you agreed to is deleted. Your medical record — readings, doses, documents and meals that form part of it — is kept, because Indian medical-records rules oblige the clinic to retain clinical records for a statutory period of at least three years. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what is kept and for how long.

How do I change what the app may do with my data?

Profile → Your choices. You can change any of it, at any time.
