Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

HIRO
Meta16labs Healthcare and Analytics Private Limited

This Privacy Policy applies to HIRO (the “App”), a companion app for people living with lupus (SLE), antiphospholipid syndrome (APS) and other conditions that involve long-term medication, including anticoagulants. HIRO is operated by Meta16labs Healthcare and Analytics Private Limited.

HIRO holds health information, which is sensitive personal data. This policy sets out exactly what we collect, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do with it.

HIRO is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. It records what you enter and shows it back to you and, where you have a care team, to them. It does not diagnose, does not tell you what dose to take, and does not tell you what to eat. If something needs attention now, contact your clinician or your local emergency number.

What we collect

Everything below is collected because the App cannot do its job without it. Each item is linked to your account, because a medical record that is not attached to a person is not a medical record.

What we do not collect

These are worth stating plainly, because health apps commonly do all of them and HIRO does not:

  • No location. The App never asks for location permission and does not use location services for any purpose, including the emergency screen.
  • No advertising. There is no advertising identifier (IDFA), no App Tracking Transparency prompt, and no advertising or marketing use of anything you record.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. The App contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting or attribution libraries. Nothing you record is shared with a data broker or combined with third-party data.
  • No Apple Health. HIRO does not read from or write to HealthKit.
  • No sale of data. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for profiling or automated decisions about you.

Permissions the App asks for

  • Camera — only when you choose to photograph a lab report, prescription, medicine or meal.
  • Photo library — only when you choose an existing photo to send.
  • Bluetooth — only to connect to your own blood pressure monitor, thermometer, oximeter or scale, so a reading can fill in without typing.
  • Notifications — optional, and only if you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we do not send push notifications from our servers. A reminder never names a medicine or a dose, so nothing sensitive appears on your lock screen.

The App works without any of these. Declining them does not stop you recording anything by hand.

Who can see your information

You

Everything you record is visible to you in the App.

Your care team

If your account is connected to a clinic, clinicians at that clinic can see what you record, and use it when caring for you. You control this: Profile → Your choices in the App lets you see what the App may do with what you record, and change it at any time. Withdrawing consent stops new clinical records being written.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers who process data strictly on our instructions:

  • A messaging provider, to deliver your one-time sign-in code over WhatsApp. It receives your phone number and the code, and nothing else.
  • A cloud hosting and database provider, which stores the records described above. Documents and photos you send are stored in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1).

We do not give any provider permission to use your information for their own purposes.

Nobody else

We disclose information outside the above only where the law requires it of us, and we will tell you where we are permitted to.

Where your information is kept, and how it is protected

  • Data held on your phone is stored in an encrypted database, with the key held in the device keychain. This is what lets the App work with no signal — readings you record offline are saved on the phone first and sent when a connection returns.
  • Data held on our servers is stored in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1) and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) only. Nothing is sent over an unencrypted connection.
  • Access by our staff is restricted and audited. Reading a patient's record is itself recorded.
  • The key that decrypts the data on your phone is held in the device keychain in a class that never leaves that device and is never carried into an iCloud backup. Restoring a backup onto another phone does not carry your key with it, so the data cannot be read there.

How long we keep it, and what deletion means

This section is exact, because the App tells you the same thing when you close your account and the two must agree.

Closing your account (Profile → Close your account) removes your sign-in from every device immediately. You will not be able to sign in again.

Your medical record is kept, because the law requires it to be. Indian medical-records rules oblige the clinic to retain clinical records for a statutory period — at least three years — so closing your account does not erase readings, doses, documents or meals that form part of that record. Data that is not part of the clinical record, and that we hold only for optional purposes you agreed to, is deleted when you withdraw that agreement or close your account.

We would rather say this plainly than promise to “delete everything”, which would be neither true nor lawful.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and related rules, you may:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
  • ask us to correct or complete anything inaccurate;
  • ask us to erase data we are not legally obliged to retain;
  • withdraw a consent you previously gave, at any time, including in the App;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to; and
  • complain to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve your concern.

To exercise any of these, contact our Grievance Officer below. We will respond within the period the law allows.

Children

HIRO is intended for adults managing their own long-term condition, or for a carer acting on a patient's behalf. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act treats anyone under 18 as a child and requires the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian before their personal data is processed. We do not knowingly process a child's data without that consent, and we do not use a child's data for tracking or advertising in any circumstances. If you believe we hold a child's data without the required consent, contact us and we will act on it.

Changes to this policy
